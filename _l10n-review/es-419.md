The two material es-419-introduced problems are: (a) two **broken anchor links** where the heading was translated but the in-page anchor target was left in English, and (b) a **systemic grammatical-gender bug** — the term "credits" was over-translated to "créditos" but treated as feminine-singular ("la créditos", "créditos insuficiente", "créditos agotada"), which is ungrammatical Spanish and also deviates from the glossary, which keeps `credit` in English. These should be fixed before publishing.

## 8. Naturalness / MT artifacts

### 8a. Systemic grammatical-gender bug: "credits" → "créditos" treated as feminine singular (HIGH priority)

The English source uses the (plural, English) term "credits". es-419 over-translated it to "créditos" (masculine plural in Spanish) but then agreed it as if it were a feminine singular noun (likely from the underlying Chinese 额度 / "la cuota"). The result is ungrammatical: "**la** créditos", "tu créditos **se agotó**", "créditos **insuficiente**", "créditos **agotada**". ~25 lines affected, concentrated in `agent/quota-and-billing.md`. ### 8b. Glossary inconsistency: credits vs créditos

The glossary (`glossary/terms.yaml`, id M2 ~line 1411) defines `en_form: credit` with `translation_strategy: keep_original_with_note` and `case_sensitive: true` — i.e. the canonical term is English **credits**. es-419 mixes both forms: 59 lines use `credits`, 69 lines use `créditos`, and 6 files use both within the same file:

- es-419/kimi-code/benefits.md:23 — `Cada solicitud consume credits de tu bolsa de créditos exclusiva` — both forms in one sentence.
- es-419/deep-research/quota-deducted.md:20 — `los credits se descuentan ... Kimi Code usa un fondo de créditos independiente` — both forms in one sentence.
- Also mixed within: es-419/agent/features-and-limits.md, es-419/agent/quota-and-billing.md, es-419/membership/overview.md, es-419/membership/update-rules.md.

Recommendation: standardize on the glossary form `credits` (English, lowercase) throughout, which also sidesteps the 8a gender problem.
