# How to save Deep Research outputs

Deep Research generates two types of reports, both of which can be saved:

## Text research report

<Frames
  src="./images/deep-research/download-save-01.png"
  alt="Download option"
/>

Click the “Download” or “Copy” button in the upper-right corner to save the report locally or paste it elsewhere. Reports can be exported as PDF or Word.

## Visual report

Click “Preview” → “Share” → “Copy link” → open it in your browser → right-click and select “Save as” to save it as HTML, PDF, or another format.

Visual reports also support:
- **Preview**: Click “Preview” to switch between web and mobile views;
- **Copy HTML source code**: Click Preview, switch to “Code mode”, and copy the visual report’s source code;
- **Public sharing**: Click Share to get a public sharing link.
