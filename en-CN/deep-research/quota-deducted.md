# Deep Research (深度研究) credit deductions

## Billing rules

Deep Research (深度研究) also distinguishes between “regular chat” and “task execution”. Once you select a Deep Research task, billing follows the task type.

- **Billing method**: After you select a Deep Research (深度研究) task, credit is deducted based on the task’s actual token usage. Deep Research and other membership features share one credit pool; Kimi Code uses a separate credit pool.
- **Refunds for failed tasks**: If a task fails to run successfully and does not return a valid result, no credit will be deducted for token usage.

## How to check your remaining credit

Go to Me → Manage → Subscription to view the Token credit usage for your current membership account.
