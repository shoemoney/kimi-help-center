# Deep Research Use Cases and Prompt Library
## Use Case Overview

| Scenario | Example Prompt |
|:---|:---|
| Industry/market research | Help me research the competitive landscape and major players in China's embodied robotics sector in 2025 |
| Competitor analysis | Compare and analyze the differences between Notion and Obsidian in product strategy |
| Investment due diligence | Summarize financing activities and valuation changes among China-based LLM companies over the past two years |
| Academic review | Map the development of RAG technology and its main current optimization directions |
| Policy interpretation | Interpret the impact of the latest data security regulations on Chinese companies expanding overseas |
| Personal decision-making | I want to buy a laptop suitable for video editing. Help me research the mainstream options |
