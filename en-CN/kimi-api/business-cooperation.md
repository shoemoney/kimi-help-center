# Business Partnerships and Contact Sales

Kimi API offers a range of partnership options for enterprise customers, designed for business needs across different scales and scenarios.

## How do I contact the sales team?

Please visit [platform.kimi.com/contact-sales](https://platform.kimi.com/contact-sales) and fill out the contact form. Our sales team will get in touch with you within 1–2 business days.

You can also include the following information in the form so we can respond more quickly:

- Company name and industry
- Estimated usage scale
- Main application scenarios
- Preferred partnership model

## Algorithm filing

The Kimi model has completed the required algorithm filing in China. If you need to review the filing information or obtain related materials for compliance purposes, please contact the sales team.

## Contract signing

Enterprise-verified users can sign a formal service contract with Moonshot:

- Standard service agreements and customized contracts are supported.
- After signing a contract, you can use corporate bank transfer top-ups and receive enterprise-grade SLA coverage.
- For the detailed process, please contact the sales team.

## Enterprise discounts

Enterprise customers with high usage can apply for dedicated discounts:

- Discount levels depend on committed usage and partnership term.
- Enterprise verification and contract signing are required.
- Please apply through the sales team and provide your estimated monthly usage.

## Enterprise API services

Enterprise API services provide the following on top of the standard API:

- Higher rate limits and concurrency quotas
- Dedicated technical support channels
- Customized SLA coverage
- Priority access to new features

For details, please visit [platform.kimi.com/contact-sales](https://platform.kimi.com/contact-sales) to contact the sales team.
