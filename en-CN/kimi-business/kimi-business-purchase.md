# How to Purchase Kimi Business Enterprise

## Purchase online

On your personal homepage, click "Membership Plan" and select "Get Enterprise" to choose the number of seats and complete your purchase.

<Frames
  src="./images/kimi-business-purchase/subscription-page.png"
  alt="Kimi membership subscription page - Get Enterprise"
  caption="Click 'Get Enterprise' on the Membership Plan page"
/>

<Frames
  src="./images/kimi-business-purchase/enterprise-dialog.png"
  alt="Kimi Business - choose the number of seats"
  caption="Choose the number of seats, then click 'Subscribe' to complete the purchase"
/>

<Callout type="tip">
Kimi Business starts at 5 seats with an annual subscription, and you can add more seats during the subscription period.
</Callout>

## Contact support

For enterprise-grade data privacy protection, enterprise-exclusive services (including offline enterprise contract signing), dedicated technical support, and other needs, scan the QR code to contact our official support directly.
