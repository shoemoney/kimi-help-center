# Configure WeChat for Kimi Claw

<Callout type="warning">
Only Kimi Claw instances created **after March 22, 2025** include the WeChat plugin and support one-click WeChat setup.
</Callout>

## Setup Steps

### Step 1: Open the WeChat channel

Open Kimi Claw settings, select “Chat Channels”, then select “WeChat”.

  src="./images/kimi-claw/wechat-bot-01.png"

<Frames

### Step 2: Scan the connection QR code

The page will display a WeChat connection QR code. Open WeChat and scan it.

<Callout type="tip">
If you are doing this on the same mobile phone, take a screenshot of the QR code and save it to your album first, then choose the album image in WeChat’s “Scan” to recognize it.
</Callout>

<Frames
  src="./images/kimi-claw/wechat-bot-04.png"

/>

<Frames
  src="./images/kimi-claw/wechat-bot-05.png"
  alt="Scan operation"
/>

### Step 3: Confirm the connection

After scanning the code, you will see a connection page in WeChat. Tap “Connect”.

<Callout type="warning">
- Each WeChat account can bind only one Kimi Claw. Connecting a new one will overwrite the previous Kimi Claw.
- After tapping “Connect”, **do not close the page**. Wait until the WeChat connection is complete.
- If you are operating on mobile, switch back to the Kimi App and wait for the connection to complete.
</Callout>

### Step 4: Start chatting

After the connection is complete, you can chat with Kimi Claw in WeChat. If you cannot find the chat entry, you can also ask directly in Kimi Claw.

## Notes

- Each WeChat account can bind only one Kimi Claw. Connecting a new Kimi Claw will automatically overwrite the previous binding.
- If you need to switch the bound Kimi Claw, first disconnect WeChat in the original Kimi Claw, then scan the code again in the new Kimi Claw to bind it.
