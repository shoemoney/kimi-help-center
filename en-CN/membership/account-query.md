# Membership Benefits: Activation and Status Check

After you purchase a membership, your benefits usually take effect immediately. If you run into any issues, please refer to the guidance below.

## Membership benefits not activated

If you have completed payment but your membership benefits have not been activated, try the following steps:

1. **Check the payment status**: Confirm that the payment was actually completed, and check the deduction record with your bank or payment platform. Some payment methods may be delayed, so we recommend waiting a few minutes and then refreshing the page.

<Callout type="info">
**Confirm you are using the same account**: Make sure the Kimi account you are logged in to is the same account used for payment. If you have multiple Kimi accounts, please switch accounts and check again.
</Callout>

2. **Try logging in again**: Log out of your current account and log back in. Your benefits status may refresh.
3. **Contact support**: If the steps above do not resolve the issue, please email **membership@moonshot.cn** with your payment screenshot and account information. We will handle it as soon as possible.

## Where can I check my remaining credit?

You can go directly to the [membership subscription page](https://www.kimi.com/membership/subscription) and click “My Subscriptions” to check it.

**Web version (kimi.com)**:

<Frames
  src="./images/membership/account-query-01.gif"
  alt="Credit lookup page on the web version"
/>

1. Click your avatar or “Me” in the lower-left corner of the page.
2. Go to “Settings → Subscription”.
3. You can then view your current plan information and remaining credit.

**Mobile App (iOS / Android)**:

1. Open the Kimi App and tap the “Settings” tab.
2. Go to “Settings → Membership Plan → My Subscriptions”.
3. The page will show your current plan details and credit usage.
