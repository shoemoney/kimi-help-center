# Membership Pricing and Plan Overview

Kimi offers five membership plans, including the free Adagio plan. All plans support monthly auto-renewal and annual auto-renewal subscriptions. Membership benefits include two credit (额度) pools: Kimi Code uses a separate pool, while other membership features share one pool. Credits (额度) can be allocated freely.

## Credit (额度) Details

<Callout type="info">
Membership benefits for all plans include two credit (额度) pools, calculated by token consumption. Kimi Code uses a separate credit (额度) pool, while other membership features, such as Agent, share one pool. Chat does not consume credits (额度). You do not need to manage usage for each feature separately. Higher-tier plans provide more credits (额度), supporting more frequent and more complex use cases.
</Callout>

- **Two credit (额度) pools**: Kimi Code uses a separate credit (额度) pool, while other membership features share one pool. Usage is calculated based on actual token consumption.
- **Monthly refresh**: Credits (额度) are automatically refreshed in each billing cycle. Unused credits (额度) expire at the end of the cycle and are cleared.
- **Frequency limits**: For 5-hour and weekly frequency controls, refer to the prompts shown on the page.
- **Usage priority**: The system consumes granted credits (额度) first, then plan credits (额度).

For more benefit details and plan comparisons, see [Membership Subscription Benefits Overview](/membership/membership-overview).

## Credit (额度) FAQ

### How can I check my credit (额度) usage?

- **Web**: Click your avatar in the lower-left corner → Settings → Subscription
- **APP**: Me → Membership Plan → Subscription

You can view your current credit (额度) balance as a percentage, the next refresh time, and the 10 most recent usage records, including time used, feature, and consumption percentage.

<Callout type="tip">
Usage record data may have a short delay. Please refer to the currently displayed credit (额度) balance.
</Callout>

### What happens when my credits (额度) run out?

Tasks already in progress can still be completed normally, but new tasks will show an insufficient credit (额度) prompt. You can:

- Wait for credits (额度) to refresh automatically (5-hour / weekly / monthly refresh, subject to the prompts shown on the page)
- Upgrade to a higher-tier membership plan for more credits (额度)
- Join official campaigns to receive granted credits (额度)

### Do granted credits (额度) expire?

Yes. Granted credits (额度), such as trial gifts or campaign rewards, usually have a validity period, such as 7 days or 30 days. They automatically expire after the validity period ends. The system consumes granted credits (额度) first, then plan credits (额度).

### Can I use all my credits (额度) on one feature?

Yes. Membership benefits include two credit (额度) pools: Kimi Code uses a separate pool, while other membership features, such as Agent, PPT, and Deep Research (深度研究), share one pool. You can use them as you choose.
