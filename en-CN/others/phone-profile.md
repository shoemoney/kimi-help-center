# Mobile Number and Profile Management

## Can I change my bound mobile number?

Yes. Go to:

【Kimi App】→【Me】→【Settings】→【Account Security】→【Mobile Number】→【Change Number】

Follow the prompts to verify the code sent to your old mobile number, then enter your new mobile number and verify it again.

## How can I change my bound number if the original mobile number has been deactivated?

Please email [support@moonshot.cn](mailto:support@moonshot.cn). Our support team will help you change the bound number after you provide the required proof materials.

## Not receiving the SMS verification code?

Please check the following:

1. Make sure the mobile number you entered is correct.
2. The verification code may take a few minutes to arrive. Please wait patiently.
3. If you still have not received it after more than 5 minutes, tap “Resend” to get a new code.

<Callout type="info">
**Note**: Currently, only mobile numbers from supported regions are supported. VoIP numbers and landlines are not supported at this time.
</Callout>

## How do I change my profile and avatar?

Go to:

【Kimi App】→【Me】→【Settings】→【Profile】→ Change your name, bio, or avatar →【Save】

<Callout type="info">
**Note**: You can change your avatar and name **up to 10 times within 30 days**. Please plan your changes accordingly.
</Callout>
