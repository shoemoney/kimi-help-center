# A Note on Recent Plan Changes

Dear Kimi users,

We're sorry for the confusion around our recent plan changes — a few points weren't explained clearly, and parts of the interface aren't polished yet.

First, please rest assured: **the benefits of all paid users are completely unaffected.** We've read your feedback and are working hard to act on it, and we're adding service capacity to keep your experience running smoothly. Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts with us.

Here's a summary of where things stand.

## Existing plans haven't been removed, and current users aren't affected

If you're on an existing plan, you can keep using it as usual, and it will renew automatically when it expires. Some display issues in the interface are being fixed and don't affect actual use.

## If you turned off auto-renewal, you can still renew

Many people turned off auto-renewal after subscribing — you can still renew. We're building this and will roll it out soon. If you run into any trouble in the meantime, please reach out to support.

## Current users can upgrade their plan

You can upgrade to a higher tier within the existing plans. We're building this and will roll it out soon. If you run into any trouble, please reach out to support.

## New plans aren't open for purchase yet

We've received your questions and suggestions about the new plans, and we're revising them. Because of capacity constraints, the new plans aren't open yet and we don't have a firm date — we'll let you know as soon as that changes.

Thank you for your understanding and support. If you have any other questions, feel free to reach us through [membership support](/membership/membership-contact).
